## Received

The case concerns Riksteatern's in-house film production, which Johan built up and led from 2012 to 2017. The commissioners were in the building: marketers, producers, social media managers and the communications department. In 2022 he returned as a guest to make the trailer for In memoriam.

## Preparation

Johan was head producer and ran the department. He recruited freelancers, led the team and took in the commissions. He often developed the idea and set the frame. Very often he shot the films and photographs himself. The edit was what he most often handed over to others.

The logo was animated by Johan and used to open or close the films.

## Material

The trailers make up the bulk. Männen med rosa triangel is close to Johan's heart. The collaboration with the creative team was a good one, and the trailer turned out strong.

::verk film-mannen-med-rosa-triangel-trailer film-fatta-med-cleo-och-juck-trailer film-de-redan-fralsta-trailer film-en-druva-i-solen-trailer film-en-komisk-depression-trailer

All are trailers, but made in different ways. In memoriam, from the guest engagement in 2022, required extensive script work and a long shoot, since the material was difficult to handle as a play. Bra människor is a parody of a social media format: a man behind the camera fires questions at the actor on the way in to rehearsal. It shows something other than filmed stage work. Effekten was shot on location in a closed-down hospital, with scenes from the play and the actors in a real setting. Beauty and the Beast ARENA and BASTU are extra material for the marketing of the tour.

### Social media

The Almedalen sketch was written by Riksteatern's dramaturgy department and played by the permanent ensemble. It was a contribution to the debate about the Nordic Resistance Movement being allowed to take part in Almedalen in 2017, and it reached a wide audience.

::verk v03-almedalen-sketch film-vad-far-ett-seminarium-i-almedalen-heta v09-dimensioner-bakom-kulisserna v02-allt-som-ar-vart-reportage v15-karlekstrailer v11-fatta-metoo v23-skimrande

### Run for your life

One of the largest undertakings. A climate relay in the run-up to the climate summit in Paris in 2015. A stone was carried from the far north of Sweden, Norway and Finland, where the Sámi began the run, all the way to Paris, where it was handed over to the summit. The relay was broadcast live. Johan edited highlights, trailers and interim reports from home, and filmed from a bicycle himself when the relay passed through Stockholm. A most unexpected play.

::verk v21-run-for-your-life-kompendium

## Decision

Imagination, heart and love for the good of society. That is how Johan sums up the years at Riksteatern.

The films for Tyst teater and for Cullberg were made within the same assignment. They are reported in cases of their own.
