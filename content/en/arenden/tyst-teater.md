## Received

Tyst teater is the theatre of the deaf within Riksteatern. It has since been renamed Riksteatern Crea. The assignments were part of Johan's work at Riksteatern, and the role was the same as in the other Riksteatern films.

## Preparation

Commissioners, actors and audience often had difficulty hearing. The films had to take this into account.

### Perfektion

Perfektion was created and directed by Josette Bushell Mingo. It was about wanting to be perfect even if you use a wheelchair, about the feeling of wanting to be more than you are, about the difficulty of not fitting into society, and about being good enough as you are.

The performance was streamed live to Riksteatern associations around the country, above all to a deaf audience. It was a hybrid production with five cameras. The audience in the black room sat close to the dancers and could follow the live picture on large screens at the same time. Johan managed the project and filmed, among other things with a jib arm. A freelance vision mixer cut the picture. The photographers and the live picture became part of the work.
