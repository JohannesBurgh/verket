## Received

The case was received in the spring of 2010, shortly after the return from Kolkata. After months of street photography in strong sun, a need arose for the opposite: Nordic light, directed images and a slight unease.

::verk 019-maskerna-vid-bordet 021-masker-svartvitt

## Preparation

The case officer notes that the masks recur. The children wear them as though it were the most natural thing in the world. The images are staged, but the play is genuine.

::verk 034-mosaikmasken 028-gula-regnjackan 030-i-ormbunkarna

## Material

During the autumn the work moved out into the landscape. The colours were pulled apart: a pink field, black water, a veil in the forest.

::verk 031-rosa-falt 029-lov-mellan-hander 043-sjon 042-i-morkret-vid-klippan

## Decision

The case is concluded with The gas mask, which the Office regards as a summary of the entire year.

::verk 079-gasmasken
