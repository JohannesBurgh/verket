## Received

Clio is Bonnier Education's venture into a fully digital learning resource. In 2015 Bonnier bought Denmark's largest digital educational publisher and began a Swedish version. Johan applied for the post of video producer and from 2018 worked in the international creative team, headquartered in Copenhagen. The Swedish part sat in the DN tower in Stockholm, about 90 people, some 70 of them editors.

The films were part of the same learning resource, for all school subjects and above all for years 4 to 9. Johan worked mostly with the social studies subjects.

## Preparation

Johan took in the commission, developed idea and script, filmed, directed and edited. He often worked in tandem with his colleague Staffan Erlandsson. They shot for each other, so that each could direct his own work.

Film in Clio was rarely an explanation. It was meant to widen the classroom and take the pupils out, as on a study visit with believers in the film about Hinduism. It was also used to spark motivation, with intro films that invited the pupils on a fictional journey through a unit of study.

## Material

Spies in the east is the intro to an English unit in which the pupils played spies in East Germany during the Cold War over several lessons. They had different roles and different information, which they could only coax out of each other by speaking English. In this film they receive their mission. Then came 360 images with clues and interactive films with different endings.

Fotografiska's kitchen in 360° is a virtual study visit to Fotografiska's organic kitchen, made in collaboration with Fotografiska. It was recorded with a rented 8K 360 camera, the latest of its time, just as Oculus had released its first version for film. The intro was shot with a 360 camera under Johan's drone.

#metoo, the tennis coach is based on authentic accounts. They were read by actors and set to slow drone shots and steady dolly moves from places where it could have happened. The anchoring in reality makes them especially affecting. Ninth-grade pupils worked with four to six such accounts, led by the teacher, in a unit on metoo and the consent law.
