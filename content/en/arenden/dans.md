## Received

Cullberg was housed in the same theatre building as Riksteatern, and the films for Cullberg were made within the same assignment. Bella Figura and In the Middle, Somewhat Elevated were two works on the same evening at the Royal Swedish Ballet in 2021.

## Preparation

Johan filmed, produced and edited. They were his productions, from start to finish. The rehearsal trailers were shot during rehearsals, the trailers on a dedicated occasion once the work was finished. At the Royal Swedish Ballet he was allowed everywhere. The trust was considerable.

There is also a three-camera recording of the whole of Plateau Effect. It is not public.

## Material

### Cullberg

Plateau Effect was powerful new dance, groundbreaking at the time, choreographed by Jefta van Dinther, who commuted between Stockholm and Berlin.
