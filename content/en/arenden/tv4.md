## Received

A freelance assignment as segment producer for the production company Tre Vänner. Snurra flaskan (Spin the Bottle) had six participants. They challenged each other with absurd dares, and whoever the bottle pointed at when it stopped had to carry out the dare. Henrik is one of the participants.

## Preparation

Johan was segment producer and additional camera. Most of it happened on the spot.

## Material

The national anthem was performed at the Swedish floorball championships in Solna. Participant Erik Barr got the dare, and the team arranged with the organisers for him to go up. There are many more segments. These three are the ones Johan has chosen to show.

All three are found to be good.
